# KMITL portfolio alignment
This project connects thematically with Biopharmaceutical Engineering through molecular biology, gene expression and molecular diagnostic technologies; bioinformatics through structured genetic information and uncertainty; biomedical imaging through segmentation and modality limits; Healthcare Information Technology through accessible interfaces, privacy, static delivery and software verification; engineering design through requirements and iterative validation; and experimental data analysis through reproducible metrics and failure analysis.

These are thematic connections, not verified claims about current KMITL courses, admissions requirements, course codes, or specific curriculum content. Official curriculum verification remains pending.

Aeralis focuses on accessible movement education and rehabilitation technology for people living with motor impairments. GENORA focuses on developmental biology, prenatal screening, genetics, molecular diagnostics and biomedical image analysis. Together they demonstrate interest in biomedical technologies supporting people and families across stages of life.
