# Results, limitations and ethics
Only outputs produced by an executed pipeline may be reported as results. See research/results/ for actual synthetic outputs when generated and docs/QA.md for verification status. Real-data evaluation is incomplete; no real performance figures or clinical conclusions are claimed.

Known errors: overlap merges components, noise creates fragments, thresholding is sensitive to illumination and contrast, morphology may delete small objects. Reference masks and counts can disagree on overlapping instances. Foreground IoU does not prove correct chromosome identification.

Scientific limits: CP is heterogeneous and not generally chromosomal; screening is probabilistic; diagnostic assays are selective; imaging cannot comprehensively inspect genetic sequence. Epigenetic association is not causation. No individual diagnosis, treatment recommendation, or pregnancy-continuation advice is provided.

Medical copy is editorial-review-ready, not clinician reviewed. URL audit and authoritative source recency require continued maintenance. Genuine galleries await provenance and licensing. WCAG 2.2 AA is a target, not a certified claim. Further work: authentic datasets, dual annotation, independent evaluation, source updates, cross-browser and assistive-technology testing, multilingual support.
